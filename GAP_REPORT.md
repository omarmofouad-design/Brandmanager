## 1. Assumptions and judgement calls

Every one of these was my call, not yours. Reverse any of them and tell me.

1. **Files were pulled from Google Drive, not the project folder.** The brief
   said "look in this folder and its subfolders". Nothing was there. Rather than
   deliver 37 blank cards, I searched the connected Drive account, found all
   seven files, and used them. If those Drive copies are not the controlled
   versions, this whole tool is invalid.

2. **"Approved response" means an objection→response structure in a source
   file.** Where a deck contains a scripted answer to an objection, I
   transcribed it. Where a deck contains only *data* on the topic, I marked the
   card **NO APPROVED RESPONSE ON FILE**, listed the data under "What is on
   file", and said explicitly on the card that the figures are approved data
   and not an approved rebuttal. I did not assemble arguments out of data
   slides. This is why 10 of 37 cards carry the no-response flag.

3. **Objections are phrased as a physician would say them,** not as the decks
   head their slides. The *responses* are transcribed; only the objection
   wording is mine. Where a deck's own objection wording was already natural
   (e.g. "BRUKINSA as a continuous therapy is not as appealing as
   fixed-duration"), I kept it verbatim.

4. **The "PREDATES AMPLIFY, VERIFY BEFORE USE" flag is applied to every
   category, not just Fixed duration.** Your instruction said "flag any card
   whose only source predates 2025". I read that as written. Six cards carry it.

5. **Files with no printed date are dated by their newest internal evidence,
   and that is stated on the card rather than hidden.** `Objection hadler` has
   no date or code; it cites AMPLIFY (NEJM 2025) and was last modified January
   2026, so it is treated as post-AMPLIFY and *not* flagged — but every card
   sourced from it carries a note that the file has no approval code.

6. **The two medical decks (ITC, Pooled Safety) are marked non-promotional on
   every card that uses them.** Both state in their own disclaimers that they
   are not for promotional use. Reps will otherwise read a citation as
   permission.

7. **Splitting rather than merging.** Where two files answer the same objection
   differently I built one card and surfaced the conflict on it, rather than
   silently picking a winner. Where a source's answer is materially old
   (October 2024 acalabrutinib response) I kept it as its own flagged card and
   built a separate, current card next to it.

8. **AMPLIFY-era safety figures are quoted exactly as the decks state them,**
   including the AZ/competitor "proof points" the decks reproduce. I did not
   round, soften, or restate a single number. Where a deck reports a figure
   without a confidence interval, none was added.

9. **WM data is quarantined.** `Zanubrutinib data in WM.pdf` supports exactly
   one card, that card is labelled WM-not-CLL, and no WM figure appears on any
   CLL card. Likewise BGB-3111-215 (mixed CLL/WM/MCL/MZL) is labelled as
   mixed-histology on its card.

10. **Category assignment is mine.** Efficacy 6, Safety and tolerability 7,
    Fixed duration 7, Comparative and ITC 5, Access and cost 3, Dosing and
    administration 3, Other 6. Cards were assigned by the *objection's* subject,
    not the data's — e.g. "fixed duration is cheaper" sits under Fixed duration
    because that is where a rep will look for it, with a cost twin under Access
    and cost.

11. **`ZANU ALL INDICATIONS.pptx` was not opened.** The brief offered
    "`.pdf` or `.pptx`"; the PDF was read in full. If the PPTX carries speaker
    notes the PDF does not, that content is not represented here.

## 4. Places where two source files answer the same objection differently

### 4.1 The MAIC of BRUKINSA vs AV — two different hazard ratios ⚠️ **most serious**

Same objection ("how does BRUKINSA compare with acalabrutinib + venetoclax"),
two files, two different analyses, two different numbers. Both are in circulation.

> **Objection Handler AMPLIFY.pptx, slide 13 (April 2026):**
> "MAIC of fit patients in 1L CLL: 55% reduced risk of progression or death with
> BRUKINSA vs. AV (**HR: 0.45; 95% CI: 0.23, 0.88; p=0.0197**)"
> "3-year PFS: **89.2%** with BRUKINSA vs. **78.9%** with AV"
> — sourced to Shadman M, et al. *Blood Adv.* 2026.

> **Zanubrutinib Indirect Treatment Comparisons, slides 49–50 (July 2025):**
> "Population-adjusted INV-PFS for zanubrutinib vs AV indicated superior INV-PFS
> in favor of zanubrutinib with **HR of 0.26 (95% CI: 0.13-0.54; P=.0003)**"
> "The 36-month PFS rate for zanubrutinib was 85.6% before matching and **88.5%**
> after matching in the base case, compared with **76.5%** for AV"
> — sourced to Munir T, et al. EHA 2025; PS1581.

These are **not** the same analysis restated. Different first authors, different
publications, different effect sizes (55% vs 74% risk reduction), different
comparator PFS (78.9% vs 76.5%). The ITC deck's own sensitivity range spans
HR 0.15 to HR 0.73 (P=.4992 at the top end) — i.e. one scenario shows no
significant difference at all.

**Handled in the tool by:** a single card presenting both, clearly labelled by
date and file, with an explicit instruction to use the April 2026 commercial
figures and never present both. **Needs an MRC ruling on which is the field
number.**

### 4.2 Median age at CLL diagnosis, and the AMPLIFY age cut-off

> **Objection hadler.pdf, slide 3 (undated):**
> "Median age of patients in the AMPLIFY trial was 61 years (versus the median
> age at CLL diagnosis is **72 years old**)"
> "**73.2%** of patients in the AMPLIFY trial were **≤65** years old"

> **Objection Handler AMPLIFY.pptx, slide 15 (April 2026):**
> "the median age of CLL diagnosis is **70 years**, with treatment typically
> initiating between the ages of ~72 and 78 years"
> "**73.2%** of patients enrolled in AMPLIFY were aged **<65** years"

Two discrepancies. First, 72 vs 70 years for median age at diagnosis — the
April 2026 deck separates *diagnosis* (70) from *treatment initiation* (72–78);
the undated file collapses them into a single "72 at diagnosis". Second, the
identical 73.2% figure is attributed to **≤65** in one file and **<65** in the
other. One of them is wrong. The April 2026 deck cites Brown, NEJM 2025 directly
and is the safer version.

**Handled in the tool by:** using the April 2026 wording on the generalisability
card, with a note on the older card pointing at the discrepancy.

### 4.3 BRUKINSA vs acalabrutinib — 48% vs 32% risk reduction

> **GCC Handler V5.0, slide 11 (October 2024):**
> "In KHAN NMA presented in EHA 2022 Brukinsa showed superiority against both
> Acala and Ibrutinib in PFS (**48% RR of progression or death against Acala**)"

> **Indirect Treatment Comparisons, slide 26 (July 2025):**
> ALPINE vs ASCEND MAIC, base case adjusted:
> "HR PFS-INV zanubrutinib vs. acalabrutinib … **0.68 (0.46-0.99, P=0.0448)**"
> (unadjusted: 0.77, 0.55–1.07, **P=0.1213** — not significant)

A 48% reduction and a 32% reduction, for the same comparison, from two different
indirect analyses. The ITC deck additionally documents a **third** result: the
prior Kittai et al MAIC (Am J Hematol 2023) "showed **similar efficacy** for
zanubrutinib and acalabrutinib (PFS-INV)", which the deck rebuts on
methodological grounds. So the source set contains three answers: better by 48%,
better by 32%, and no different.

**Handled in the tool by:** keeping the October 2024 response as its own flagged
card carrying the conflict warning, and building a separate current card on the
2025 MAIC that reproduces its caveats verbatim.

### 4.4 Discontinuation rate in the pooled population — 4.8% vs 13.6%

Both figures describe the same N=1,550 patients.

> **Objection Handler AMPLIFY.pptx, slide 14 (April 2026):**
> "Of the 1,550 patients treated with BRUKINSA across five B-cell malignancies,
> **4.8% discontinued treatment due to AEs** and 5.0% underwent dose reductions"
> — sourced to BRUKINSA SmPC (EU), November 2025.

> **Pooled Safety Data, slide 59 (May 2025):**
> All zanubrutinib (N=1550): TEAEs "Leading to treatment discontinuation …
> **211 (13.6%)**"
> — sourced to Brown J et al. *Haematologica* 2024.

Almost certainly different denominators of intent (SmPC adverse *reactions* vs
all treatment-emergent AEs), but the decks do not say so, and the two numbers
differ by a factor of nearly three on the same population. A rep who quotes
4.8% and is then shown 13.6% has a credibility problem.

**Handled in the tool by:** presenting both on the long-term-tolerability card
with an explicit instruction to quote one or the other with its source and never
side by side. **Needs a one-line MRC clarification of what each measures.**

### 4.5 Indication count — "5 in FDA and 4 locally" vs "five B-cell malignancies"

> **GCC Handler V5.0, slide 14 (October 2024):**
> "Brukinsa is approved in **5 different indications in FDA and 4 locally**"

> **Objection Handler AMPLIFY.pptx, slide 14 (April 2026):**
> "1,550 patients treated with BRUKINSA across **five B-cell malignancies**"
> — BRUKINSA SmPC (EU), November 2025.

Not a direct contradiction, but the October 2024 "4 locally" is a Gulf-specific
regulatory count that is two years old and cannot be assumed current. A third
count sits in `ZANU ALL INDICATIONS.pdf` ("Approved in 70 countries/regions"),
sourced to an **August 2023** corporate presentation.

### 4.6 The PPI claim is self-qualified and unsupported

> **GCC Handler V5.0, slide 11 (October 2024):**
> "Flexible dosing and not impacted by PPIs **"In case of Capsule"**"

No PK data, no study, no figures anywhere in the seven files. Meanwhile
`ZANU ALL INDICATIONS.pdf` states the formulation as "160mg BID or 320mg QD PO
(**80 mg capsules — tablet formulation in development**)". So the only PPI claim
on file is scoped to a formulation that the same source set describes as being
superseded. **Do not extend this claim to a tablet.**

### 4.7 del(17p) PFS — 72.2% and 64%, different timepoints

> **ZANU ALL INDICATIONS.pdf, SEQUOIA Arm C 5-year section (ASCO 2025 abstract
> 7011, data cutoff 30 April 2024):**
> "The estimated **60-month PFS** with zanubrutinib was **72.2%**"

> **Objection Handler AMPLIFY.pptx, slides 13/15/16 (April 2026), and
> Objection hadler.pdf slide 5:**
> "6-year PFS del(17p): **64%** (95% CI: 53.6, 72.8) with BRUKINSA at a mFU of
> **76.7 months**"

**This is not a contradiction** — 60 months vs 72 months, different cutoffs — but
the two numbers are close enough, and quoted loosely enough across the decks,
that they will be mixed up in the field. Listed here so it is on the record.

**Handled in the tool by:** a note on the del(17p) card stating both timepoints
and instructing that they never be quoted together.

## 9. Change log — figures derived in the Sales module (6 September 2026)

The Sales module now shows three numbers the ALLnONE file does not state
itself. Every other figure in the tool is the file's own, reproduced as
recorded. These three are arithmetic on the file's figures, and each is
labelled in the interface so no one mistakes it for a source figure:

| Derived figure | How it is worked out | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **% of target** | actual ÷ target for the same measure, month and account, using the pair the file itself defines (patients / patient target, units / target units, value / target value) | stat tiles, and the "against target" chart |
| **Months of cover** | ending stock at the last month with data ÷ the mean of the months of consumption recorded to date | ending-stock tile, stock chart |
| **Share of therapies** | Brukinsa patients recruited ÷ the sum of every therapy column (Brukinsa + Acala CLL + Acala MCL + A+V + Ibrutinib mono + I+V + Pirtubrutinb + Ibrutinib) | its own tile |

**A caveat on share of therapies.** The file's therapy columns are not all the
same kind of number. `Brukinsa patient recruited` accumulates across the year
(the file's own year-to-date rule for it is a Jan–Jul sum), while several
competitor columns hold a patient count as at each month (their year-to-date
rule is the July figure, not a sum). Dividing one by the other mixes a flow
by a stock, so the percentage is indicative of relative presence, not a
market share as a market-research house would define it. It is not a figure
to quote externally. The underlying columns are shown unaltered in the
"Patients by therapy" chart and in the account table, which is where the
defensible reading is.

Therapy column names are reproduced exactly as the file spells them,
`Pirtubrutinb` included. Nothing is renamed, merged or recalculated.

**Account field notes** were added: dated, attributed to the signed-in user,
held in that browser only, and carried into the Export notes file alongside
the objection notes.
