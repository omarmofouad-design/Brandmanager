Found and deliberately excluded

Manufacturer-produced material located in the project Drive. BeOne Medicines markets zanubrutinib, one of the agents this tool must compare impartially, so none of this can be a source of record for a treatment-selection rule — regardless of whether its content is accurate. Listed here so the exclusion is auditable rather than silent.

    The last item is the instructive one. It bears directly on node 11, which is otherwise thinly sourced — so it is tempting. But it is a conference abstract, retrospective and propensity-matched rather than randomised, and it favours the product of the company holding the file, while head-to-head randomised evidence for the same question exists (elevate-rr-jco-2021, alpine-nejm-2023).

    Source vintage — publication year of each register entry

    Each mark is one source. Staleness is a property of guidance, not of literature — RESONATE-2 being from 2015 is history, not decay, and it will never be reissued. So the signal is the oldest guidance mark: iwcll-2018, eight years old and still the presumptive authority for treatment initiation. Whether the 2026 EHA guideline changes that is conflict C-06. Three drug labels have no recoverable revision date and sit off the scale.
